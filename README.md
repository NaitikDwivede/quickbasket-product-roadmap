# QuickBasket — Feature Prioritization & Product Roadmap

> Note: QuickBasket is a fictional quick-commerce app created for this Product Management portfolio case study. All user feedback, customer volumes, RICE scores, KPIs, and business assumptions are synthetic and illustrative.

## Project Overview

QuickBasket is a quick-commerce grocery delivery app designed for busy urban customers who need essential items delivered quickly and reliably.

This project demonstrates how a Product Manager can turn competing customer requests into a focused roadmap. I analyzed 18 feature ideas, applied the RICE prioritization framework and MoSCoW method, and created a quarterly product roadmap with stakeholder trade-offs, success metrics, dependencies, and recommendations.

## Business Problem

Quick-commerce customers often face uncertainty after placing an order:

- Items may be unavailable.
- Substitutions may not match their preferences.
- Delivery status may be unclear.
- Reordering regular groceries can take too much time.
- Support resolution can be slow when an item is missing.

The product challenge was to decide which features should be built first with limited engineering capacity.

## Key Product Decisions

### Q1: Build trust in the core order experience

Prioritized:

- Live order tracking
- Substitution preferences
- One-tap reorder
- Real-time availability badges

These features were selected because they improve order confidence, reduce customer-support contacts, and make repeat purchases easier.

### Q2: Make planned shopping easier

Prioritized:

- Missing-item self-service refunds
- Saved shopping lists
- Delivery instructions
- Dietary and allergen filters
- Scheduled delivery slots

### Q3–Q4: Test retention and exploration opportunities

Potential experiments include:

- Price-drop alerts
- Delivery-pass membership
- Smart replenishment suggestions
- In-app support chat
- Product ratings and reviews
- Loyalty concepts

These were intentionally not prioritized before core fulfillment reliability and customer trust.

## KPI Framework

| KPI | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Order contact rate | Measure whether tracking and refunds reduce customer support requests |
| Repeat purchase rate | Measure whether reorder and saved-list features increase retention |
| Substitution acceptance rate | Measure fulfillment quality after preference controls |
| On-time delivery rate | Protect the core quick-commerce service promise |
| Contribution margin per order | Ensure retention experiments remain financially sustainable |

## Key Learning

A high RICE score should inform prioritization, but should not automatically decide the roadmap. Operational dependencies, data reliability, customer trust, and financial risk must also be considered before committing product scope.
